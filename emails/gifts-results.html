*|IF:GIFT1=r_prophecy|*

Romans 12 · Speaking gift

Prophetic Perception

“Having gifts differing according to the grace that was given to us: if prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of our faith.” Romans 12:6 (WEB)

What it is

In Romans 12, prophecy is a gift of grace that shapes how you see. You notice what God is doing and what He is saying, and you feel a strong pull to speak truth, call people back to God and stand for what is right.

How it shows up

  • You sense quickly when something is off spiritually or when God is moving.
  • You care more about truth than comfort, and you want people to walk in it.
  • You often pray before you speak, and your words land with weight.

How to grow in it

  • Let love lead. Prophecy is for strengthening, encouraging and comforting people (1 Corinthians 14:3).
  • Measure what you sense by Scripture and share it with gentle language.
  • Pray more than you say. Many prophetic people do their best work in intercession.

*|ELSEIF:GIFT1=r_serving|*

Romans 12 · Serving gift

Serving

“Or service, let us give ourselves to service.” Romans 12:7 (WEB)

What it is

Serving is the grace to see a practical need and meet it with joy. People with this gift make the work of the church possible. It is the towel Jesus picked up in John 13, and it is never small in the Kingdom.

How it shows up

  • You notice what needs to be done before anyone asks.
  • You find real joy in behind-the-scenes work.
  • You would rather help than be thanked.

How to grow in it

  • Serve from sonship, not for approval. You are already loved.
  • Build rhythms of rest so serving stays a joy and not a burden.
  • Invite someone to serve alongside you and pass on what you know.

1 Corinthians 12 · Speaking gift

Word of Knowledge

“And to another the word of knowledge, according to the same Spirit.” 1 Corinthians 12:8 (WEB)

What it is

A word of knowledge is a piece of information the Spirit reveals about the past or present that you could not have known naturally. It is a fragment, not the whole picture. Jesus told the woman at the well about her five husbands, and one sentence opened her heart (John 4:18).

How it shows up

  • As you pray, a name, a body part or a situation comes to mind.
  • You get impressions that turn out to be specific and true.
  • You gently ask, "Does this mean anything to you?"

How to grow in it

  • Value small impressions. One can be the key that opens a door.
  • Use gentle language. If you miss it, no one gets hurt.
  • Don't fear missing it. The bigger risk is never stepping out.

Working of Miracles

“And to another workings of miracles.” 1 Corinthians 12:10 (WEB)

A miracle is a supernatural intervention in the ordinary course of nature, usually instant. The gift of faith kept Paul safe in the storm. The working of miracles is Jesus rebuking the storm so it stopped.

  • Give God the glory, every time it happens.

1 Corinthians 12 · Speaking gift

Prophecy

“But he who prophesies speaks to men for their edification, exhortation, and consolation. (1 Corinthians 14:3)” 1 Corinthians 12:10 (WEB)

What it is

Prophecy is speaking a message from God to strengthen, encourage and comfort people. Paul tells us to earnestly desire it because it builds up the church, and he says everyone can prophesy (1 Corinthians 14:1, 31).

How it shows up

  • You sense God's heart for people.

How to grow in it

  • Earnestly desire it, and start in small ways.
  • Don't force it. Stay open to the Spirit.
  • Submit words to Scripture and to trusted leaders.

*|ELSEIF:GIFT1=c_discern|*

1 Corinthians 12 · Speaking gift

Discerning of Spirits

“And to another discerning of spirits.” 1 Corinthians 12:10 (WEB)

What it is

Discerning of spirits is insight into the spiritual realm: perceiving whether what is at work is the Holy Spirit, a human spirit or an evil spirit. It is more than reading people. Paul discerned the spirit behind the slave girl in Philippi and set her free (Acts 16:16-18).

How it shows up

  • You sense the spiritual atmosphere of a room.
  • You can tell when something sounds right but is not of God.
  • You know how to pray because you see what is going on.

How to grow in it

  • Let discernment lead you to prayer, not suspicion.
  • Grow in the Word so you can test what you sense.
  • Stay humble and share what you see with care.

Tongues

“He who speaks in another language edifies himself. (1 Corinthians 14:4)” 1 Corinthians 12:10 (WEB)

Tongues is speaking in a language you have not learned, whether a human language or the tongues of angels. A private prayer language builds you up (1 Corinthians 14:2-4). A message in tongues for the church should be interpreted.

Apostle (Pioneer)

“He gave some to be apostles... for the perfecting of the saints, to the work of serving, to the building up of the body of Christ.” Ephesians 4:11 (WEB)

  • Let your community affirm your calling over time.

Discerning of Spirits

Discerning of spirits is insight into the spiritual realm: perceiving whether what is at work is the Holy Spirit, a human spirit or an evil spirit. It is more than reading people. Paul discerned the spirit behind the slave girl in Philippi and set her free (Acts 16:16-18).

  • Grow in the Word so you can test what you sense.
