The Songs of Ascent.

The meaning of Psalms 120 to 134, the pilgrim songs Israel sang on the climb to Jerusalem, from the Incense & Oil series at GateCity Buckhead.

What are the Songs of Ascent?

The Songs of Ascent are Psalms 120 to 134, fifteen short songs that pilgrims sang on the way up to Jerusalem for the great feasts. Each one carries the title "A Song of Ascents." Read together, they trace one journey: from distress far from home (Psalm 120) to blessing in the house of the Lord (Psalm 134).
