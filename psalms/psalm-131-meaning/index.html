Psalm 131 Meaning: A Quieted Soul

A Song of Ascents · David

The short answer

Psalm 131 is a three-verse Song of Ascents by David about humility and rest in God. David says his heart is not proud and he does not chase things too great for him; instead he has calmed and quieted his soul like a weaned child with its mother. It teaches that stillness and humility are the ground of hope in God.

Incense & Oil, Part 3: A Quieted Soul

Psalm 131 (World English Bible)

1 Yahweh, my heart isn’t haughty, nor my eyes lofty; nor do I concern myself with great matters, or things too wonderful for me.

2 Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul, like a weaned child with his mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me.

3 Israel, hope in Yahweh, from this time forward and forever more.

What Psalm 131 means

"Yahweh, my heart isn't haughty, nor my eyes lofty; nor do I concern myself with great matters, or things too wonderful for me." David, a king with every reason to grasp for more, lays down the need to control what is beyond him.

"Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul, like a weaned child with his mother." The picture is precise. A nursing infant comes to its mother hungry and demanding. A weaned child no longer comes only to get something. It simply rests with her. That is a soul at peace with God: not empty of desire, but content to be with Him.

The psalm ends by turning outward: "Israel, hope in Yahweh, from this time forward and forever more."

Where it fits in the Songs of Ascent

After the long wait of Psalm 130 comes stillness. Humility and hope belong together, and this is the posture the whole climb has been forming. Stillness is not the absence of prayer. It is the ground of it.

A question to sit with

What would it look like to come to God this week without asking for anything, just to be with Him?

A prayer you can pray

Lord, I lay down the things too great for me. Quiet my soul like a weaned child resting with its mother. I don't come to get something. I come to be with You. My hope is in You, now and forever. In Jesus' name, amen.
