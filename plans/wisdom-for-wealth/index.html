Wisdom for Wealth.

Mastering money God's way

Jesus ties your treasure directly to your heart. In six days, Pastor Hazen Stevens walks through what Scripture says about money, so you can hold it with open hands and live with freedom instead of worry.

  • How the love of money can quietly pull us away from faith
  • What putting God first looks like with your finances
  • How generosity and the tithe can ease money anxiety
  • Three simple principles for earning, saving, and giving

For anyone who wants a biblical, practical way to think about money.
