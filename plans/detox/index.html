Free Bible Plan · 21 days

Detox.

21 days to a holy and healthy life

Questions this Bible plan answers
  • How do I break free from an addiction or a habit I can't stop?
  • What does the Bible say about strongholds and spiritual bondage?
  • How do I find real freedom in Christ?
  • How do I resist temptation?
  • How do I reset my spiritual life when I feel stuck or dry?
  • What does a holy and healthy life look like?

Something in your life may be hurting you without you noticing, and something good may be missing. Over 21 days, Pastor Hazen Stevens guides you through five steps toward a holy and healthy life.

  • Reflect until you see clearly
  • Remove what is harmful
  • Replace it with something better
  • Stay connected to God
  • Encourage the people your life touches

For anyone ready for a reset in body, mind, and spirit. Pairs with the book Detox.
