Preparing for Outpouring.

A 10-day journey toward Pentecost and the Holy Spirit

Whether you are a seasoned believer or just beginning to explore, this ten-day journey makes room for the Holy Spirit. Each day offers space to slow down, reflect, and ask God to fill you again.

  • Who the Holy Spirit is and what He does
  • Making room for God's presence
  • Asking to be filled with the Spirit
  • Preparing your heart for a fresh outpouring

For anyone hungry to know the Holy Spirit and be filled with His power.
