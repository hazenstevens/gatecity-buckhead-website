Free Bible Plan · 10 days

Conversations.

Prayer and the sacred gift of our words

Prayer is a conversation with God, and our words carry spiritual power. This ten-day devotional explores prayer, encouragement, cross-cultural friendship, and sharing your faith, following the example of Jesus.

  • Learning to talk with God, not just at Him
  • Speaking with intention, humility, and love
  • Words that build up and encourage
  • Conversations that cross cultures and share the gospel

For anyone who wants a richer prayer life. Pairs with the book Conversations.
