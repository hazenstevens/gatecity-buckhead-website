Free Bible Plan · 8 days

Culture of Hunger.

A steady hunger for God

As the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul longs for You (Psalm 42:1). This eight-day plan helps you build a lasting hunger for God, not a one-time spark.

  • Seeking God's presence
  • Engaging deeply with Scripture
  • Valuing holiness
  • Expecting God's power and pressing for breakthrough
  • Keeping your passion through every season

For anyone who feels spiritually dry and wants to want God again.
