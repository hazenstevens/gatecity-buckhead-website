Message Series · 8 Messages · How the gospel changes you

Transformed.

The gospel is not only a belief system. It is a transforming reality. This series covers new birth, the simple gospel, intimacy with God, grace and faith, and the redemption of all things.

Questions this series answers
  • What does it mean to be born again?
  • What is the simple gospel?
  • Are we saved by grace or by works?
  • What happens when we die?
