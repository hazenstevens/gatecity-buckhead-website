Message Series · 8 Messages · The Holy Spirit

Manifest.

Who is the Holy Spirit, and what does He do in you? In this eight-part series, Pastor Hazen Stevens and our team teach that the Holy Spirit is a person, not a power, and walk through His fruit, His gifts, the five-fold ministry, unity, and boldness.

Questions this series answers
  • Who is the Holy Spirit?
  • What are the gifts and fruit of the Spirit?
  • How do I pray with boldness?
  • What does unity in the church look like?
