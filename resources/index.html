The Desire to Go Up

In this first blog of Incense & Oil, Pastor Hazen opens Psalm 122 and the Songs of Ascent, the pilgrim songs Israel sang on the climb to Jerusalem. From a knock on the door before dawn to the gates of the city, he traces the whole journey in nine verses: a joyful invitation, unified praise, prayer with authority, and shalom that flows from our hearts to our homes to Atlanta, all for the sake of the house of the Lord.

September 20, 2026 · Hazen Stevens

Stand

In the final message of our Family Matters series, Pastor Hazen asks why a letter about family ends with armor. Walking through Ephesians 6, he retells Baudelaire's Generous Gambler and the stand at Thermopylae, tours the Roman armor an Ephesian would have recognized piece by piece, from the cingulum to the gladius, and lands on Paul's real conclusion: be strong in the Lord, put on the whole armor, pray without ceasing, and when the evil day has done its worst, be found standing.

September 11, 2026 · Hazen Stevens

Light in the Lord

In this blog, Pastor Hazen continues at the summit of Ephesians 5, where Paul tells the church not that they walked into the light but that they are light in the Lord. Tracing the golden thread of light through Scripture, from "Let there be light" to "you are the light of the world," he shows how God, light, and words always travel together, unpacks the Greek behind "keep on being filled with the Spirit," and lands on the surprising discovery that nearly half of Paul's instructions in this passage concern our speech.
