What Is Grace? God's Gift That Saves and Changes You

By Hazen Stevens · November 10, 2026

Adapted from Chapter 4 of my book, Transformed.

Grace is God's undeserved favor, freely given to us through Jesus. It means God gives us what we could never earn: forgiveness, a restored relationship with Him, and eternal life. But grace is more than a pardon. It is also God's power at work in us, changing us from the inside out. Grace saves us, and grace transforms us.

for by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, that no one would boast.

Ephesians 2:8-9, World English Bible

Grace Is a Gift, Not a Wage

Everything else in life tends to work on wages. You perform, and you are paid. You fail, and you lose. Grace breaks that pattern. Paul says we are saved by grace through faith, and that it is "not of works, that no one would boast." We do not bring God our best effort and hope it is enough. We bring Him our need, and He gives us His Son.

That is why Jesus could tell the story of a son who wasted everything and still came home to a robe, a ring and a feast (Luke 15). The son was rehearsing how to earn his way back as a servant. The father would not let him finish the speech. That is grace.

Grace Does Not Leave You the Way It Found You

Many people come to Christ expecting instant change, and some things do change in a moment. But the transformation grace brings is also a lifelong journey. The gospel does not only restore us to God. It reshapes us.

Therefore we don’t faint, but though our outward man is decaying, yet our inward man is renewed day by day.

2 Corinthians 4:16, World English Bible

Under the new covenant, the Holy Spirit lives inside every believer, renewing and regenerating us (John 3:5, John 7:37-39). We are no longer left to our own strength to overcome sin and brokenness. Grace works in at least three places:

  • Your mind. We are transformed by the renewing of our minds (Romans 12:2), replacing lies we have believed with the truth of God's Word. That happens through the Spirit, Scripture, prayer and community, not sheer willpower.
  • Your heart. Grace reaches the hidden wounds of rejection, disappointment and unforgiveness. David prayed, "Create in me a clean heart, O God" (Psalm 51:10). True transformation goes deeper than behavior.
  • Your habits. There is a real battle between the Spirit and the flesh (Galatians 5:17). Grace gives us victory, not by suppressing desires through effort, but by the Spirit replacing them with new ones.

For sin will not have dominion over you. For you are not under law, but under grace.

Romans 6:14, World English Bible

Grace Meets Us in the Light

Real change begins with an open heart. It is hard to receive grace while hiding behind a front of strength. Most of us resist being honest out of fear of rejection, judgment or exposure. But Scripture says healing is found in the light.

Confess your offenses to one another, and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The insistent prayer of a righteous person is powerfully effective.

James 5:16, World English Bible

I know this personally. For years I fought a hidden addiction, making promises to myself that I would never go back, and then finding myself there again, more defeated than before. Shame kept me silent. Everything began to change when I finally brought it into the light with trusted friends. I was not met with condemnation. I was met with grace, prayer and accountability. Over time I learned that freedom was not only about resisting temptation. It was about letting the Holy Spirit change what I wanted.

But we all, with unveiled face seeing the glory of the Lord as in a mirror, are transformed into the same image from glory to glory, even as from the Lord, the Spirit.

2 Corinthians 3:18, World English Bible

We are not transformed by striving. We are transformed by beholding. The struggle that once defined me no longer has power over me, and my story of grace is still being written.

Grace Breaks Cycles

Many of us grew up watching patterns of addiction, anger, broken marriages or fear, and no matter how hard we try to be different, we find ourselves walking the same roads. Grace offers something willpower cannot: a new beginning.

In college I walked into a large Tuesday night Bible study, sat in the balcony, and heard a pastor talk about the judgment so many of us carry toward our parents. He opened a suitcase on stage to show the baggage of unforgiveness we drag through life. I realized I was on the same path I resented in my own family. That night I prayed a simple prayer: "God, if You will be a Father to me, I want to be Your son." It was the turning point of my life.

You do not break generational cycles by trying harder. The flesh cannot defeat the flesh. Only the Spirit can make us new.

Therefore if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things have passed away. Behold, all things have become new.

2 Corinthians 5:17, World English Bible

Grace Is Greater

No matter what your past has looked like, no matter what you have done or what has been done to you, grace is greater.

The law came in besides, that the trespass might abound; but where sin abounded, grace abounded more exceedingly;

Romans 5:20, World English Bible

If you have never received God's grace, you can today. Read How to Be Saved, or start with What Is the Gospel?

Go deeper. This post is adapted from Transformed: How the Gospel Changes Everything by Hazen Stevens. Get the book on Amazon, or watch the Transformed sermon series free.
