Beholding the Mystery & Majesty

Stop and look until the wonder of Christmas undoes you.

A four-week Advent devotional with daily Scripture readings, for individuals, families, and small groups to read together. Each week is its own kind of beholding.

  • Harvest Christ coming for the whole world.
  • Home Christ filling a humble dwelling.
  • Heart Christ coming near enough to live in you.
  • Heralds Christ sending you out to carry Him further.

Each day includes Scripture, a short devotional, a personal reflection, a question to share around the table, and a prayer.
