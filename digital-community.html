Digital Community

Belong from anywhere.

Sundays anchor us, but wholehearted living is formed in the spaces in between. Whether you attend in person or engage online, these pathways are open to everyone.

Sundays · 8:15–9:00pm

Sunday Forum

Every Sunday evening we gather for our Discovery Bible Study — a space to slow down and carry the morning message a little further by exploring the passage more deeply. Through conversation, shared reflection, and prayer, we process what we heard and explore how to live it out together.

This is for anyone longing to connect — whether you attend in person, online, or are exploring community for the first time.

Virtual Prayer

Prayer is one of the heartbeats of our community. Morning and evening gatherings center on our current message series; afternoon prayer focuses on a rotating set of topical needs. Small Groups (Formation Nights)

For those longing to grow in deeper community and spiritual formation, Formation Nights offer a guided midweek space to slow down and be shaped together. We start with a short exhortation and teaching, move into discussion in your small group, and close with highlights and takeaways.

Anyone is welcome, even without signing up for the full cycle.

Discipleship Cohort

A way to truly belong, even from a distance.

The Digital Discipleship Cohort is a shared journey built around relationships, prayer, and simple rhythms that help you follow Jesus in everyday life — Christ-centered community, rhythms of prayer and formation, clear commitments, and flexible practice in your own local context.
