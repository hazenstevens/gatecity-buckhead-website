Our Mission

Follow Jesus for your whole life.

GateCity Buckhead is a church in Buckhead, Atlanta, that exists to help you follow Jesus for your whole life: through every season, into every area of your life, and toward the wholeness He promises.

Our Vision Map

5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1

Following Jesus is not meant to be vague. Our vision map, 5-4-3-2-1, gives every person at GateCity Buckhead a clear path for growing as a disciple: five behaviors to practice, four spaces to live in, three promises to hold onto, two things that make us distinct, and one purpose that ties it all together.

Five Behaviors

What we practice together.

  • PrayWe connect with God and each other through our virtual prayer teams, morning, midday, and evening. See the prayer schedule.
  • ServeSpiritual growth happens shoulder to shoulder. We will walk you through our serving areas on a Sunday morning so you can find your fit. Find your place to serve.
  • GivePartnering financially in what God is doing in Buckhead and among the nations. Opening our hands opens our hearts. Ways to give.
  • FormGrowing in faith through small groups on Wednesday nights and the Digital Discipleship Cohort, where a few committed believers journey together in prayer, formation, and Bible study. Get connected.
  • ReachLiving outwardly through digital outreach, life-on-life meetings, and inviting others to meet Jesus.
Four Core Values

The spaces where a whole life is formed.

  • The AltarLove for God. Corporate and personal prayer to encounter God.
  • The TableLove for one another. Spiritual formation through life in community.
  • The RoadLove for the world. Living on mission together, from neighborhoods to the nations (Matthew 28:18–20).
  • The Way of LoveThe posture of it all. The most excellent way (1 Corinthians 13), rooted in the Greatest Commandment (Matthew 22:37–40), the New Commandment (John 13:34), and the Second Commandment (Mark 12:31).
Three Promises

What God offers you.

  • Healthy todayJesus has given us everything we need for life and godliness (2 Peter 1:3) and promises abundant life (John 10:10): freedom from what holds you back, and wholeness in body, mind, and spirit.
  • A meaningful and impactful lifeYour life will make a difference. Your gifts and calling are meant to serve others and fulfill the purpose God has for you.
  • Eternal significance and rewardA legacy that outlasts this life, and the joy of hearing “Well done, good and faithful servant” (Matthew 25:21).
Two Distinctives

What makes us who we are.

  • Night and day worship and prayerVirtual prayer rooms meet at different times through the week as a gift to our city.
  • Reaching the unreachedWe focus on people and places around the world where the gospel has not yet been shared.
One Purpose

Christ-formed lives on mission.

“I labor until Christ is formed in you” (Galatians 4:19). Everything above serves one purpose: to live Christ-formed lives on mission. Wherever you are on that road, there is a next step for you here.
