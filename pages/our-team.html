Our Team

Meet the leadership team.

The people you'll actually run into on a Sunday.

Hazen Stevens

Lead Pastor

Hazen thrives on witnessing lives transformed by Jesus. With a background in theater and drama from his youth, he brings creativity and energy to every aspect of ministry. His free time is filled with adventure, from Topgolf to hiking, reading, and hosting friends. Hazen dreams of visiting Japan, drawn to its culture and landscapes. What he loves most about GateCity is creating meaningful experiences that connect people with God and each other.

Hannah Stevens

Co-Pastor, Spiritual Formation & Small Groups

Hannah loves creating spaces where people can slow down and deeply connect with God and others. As a former MVP of her college rugby team, she channels her passion and persistence into fostering a vibrant community at GateCity. In her free time, you'll find her hanging with her kids, sharing laughs, and making memories. If she could travel anywhere, it would be England, for tea time, picturesque streets, and rich history.

Son Byrd

Pastor of Worship & Creative Expression

Son loves creating environments where creatives can thrive and express themselves in worship. With a unique family background — he has twin and quadruplet siblings — Son knows the value of connection and collaboration. In his free time, he enjoys writing music and dreaming of visiting fashion capitals worldwide. What Son loves most about GateCity is its forward-thinking mission and the genuine desire to do ministry with excellence and authenticity.

Andrew Faletti

Pastor of Prayer & Outreach

Andrew lives to equip and empower people to grow in their faith. A professional drummer since 2006, Andrew finds joy in learning challenging songs on the guitar during his downtime. He dreams of visiting Iceland or New Zealand for their breathtaking natural beauty. What does Andrew love most about GateCity? The chance to work alongside an amazing team of dedicated individuals who share his passion for ministry.

Halima Edge

Children's Pastor, Administrator

Halima brings a unique mix of determination, heart, and passion to GateCity Buckhead. She's dedicated to ensuring everything runs smoothly while fostering meaningful connections with the people she serves. Outside of work, Halima enjoys losing herself in audiobooks and embracing her musical side. One of her lifelong dreams is to visit a vibrant country in Africa, immersing herself in its breathtaking culture and beauty.

Karissa Corpeny

Director of Systems & Processes

Karissa thrives working with a high-capacity team driven by mission and excellence. A hidden adventurer, she once caught a monster Tarpon fish with her husband — a memory she treasures. In her downtime, she enjoys mountain biking, reading, and exploring the outdoors. Her dream destination? The awe-inspiring Redwoods, a place close to her heart. Karissa loves being part of a presence-centered church where faith comes alive.

Kennah Jones

Marketing & Communications Director

Kennah is passionate about blending creativity and administration to share GateCity's vision with the world. A self-proclaimed coffee lover, she enjoys exploring new coffee shops and restaurants with friends. Fun fact: her first job involved dressing up as princesses and superheroes. If given the chance, she'd visit Switzerland for the alpine landscapes, charming villages, pristine lakes, and the chocolate.

Sarah Pearl Shivers

Associate Pastor of Prayer

Sarah Pearl is passionate about helping people connect deeply with the presence of God through prayer and worship. After starting at GateCity Lawrenceville in 2018, she served on Prayer Room staff and joined the Buckhead team in 2025. A trained dancer since childhood, she brings grace and creativity into everything she does. What she loves most about GateCity is the sense of family and the vibrant, growing prayer culture being built in the heart of Atlanta.

Angel Colon

Virtual Director & Worship Leader

Angel is passionate about creating worship spaces that transcend physical boundaries. Since joining GateCity in 2021, he has helped pioneer in-person and virtual worship experiences marked by joy, connection, and the tangible presence of the Spirit. With his Puerto Rican roots and vibrant personality, Angel brings warmth and rhythm to every set. A baker and food-lover at heart, he loves inventing new dishes and singing show tunes.
