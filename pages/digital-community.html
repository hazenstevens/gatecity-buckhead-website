Small Groups (Formation Nights)

Formation Nights are a place to build meaningful relationships with others in our church community as we learn to follow Jesus together. Whether you're brand new to GateCity Buckhead or have been around for a while, come connect, share life, and find encouragement for the journey of discipleship.

Our fall six-week series meets every Wednesday, October 14 through November 18. No special preparation or Bible knowledge required. Just come as you are.

  • Large Group DevotionalA guided reflection to help us name our hunger and orient our hearts toward God.
  • Small Group BreakoutsAbout 40 minutes of intentional conversation, listening, and prayer with others.
  • Closing TimeRegathering for shared prayer and a final word of encouragement.
  • DeviceJoin from your own charged device (it helps breakout rooms run smoothly).
  • EnvironmentFind a quiet, distraction-free space if possible.
  • BringA journal to reflect and note what God stirs in you.
