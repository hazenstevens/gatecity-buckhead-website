Are you struggling with anxiety?

You are not alone, and you are not weak or faithless for feeling it. Jesus asked His frightened friends in the middle of a storm, “Why are you so afraid?” He was not shaming them. He was showing them who was in the boat. Start here, at your own pace, for free.

21 Days of Fearless Living

Three 7-day devotionals.

Adapted from 21 Days of Fearless Living by Pastors Hazen and Hannah Stevens. Each one takes about ten minutes a day: a Scripture passage, a short devotional, a simple step to take, and a prayer. Do one week, or walk through all three.

Part 1 · 7 days

Trusting God in the Storm

Jesus asleep in the storm, singing at midnight in a prison cell, and three Psalms that have carried God’s people through fear.

Part 2 · 7 days

Courage for Anxious Hearts

Perfect love that drives out fear, Peter on the water, turning worry into prayer, and casting your cares on the God who cares for you.

Part 3 · 7 days

Free From Fear and Shame

How fear and shame work together, trusting God beyond what you can see, and why we were never meant to walk free alone.
