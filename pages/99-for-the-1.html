The heart

Leave the 99. Go after the one.

This campaign was born from the parable of the shepherd who leaves the 99 to pursue the one. We believe God is calling us to create a space for those who are far from Him to encounter His love — to go after the one.

“If a man has a hundred sheep and one of them goes astray, does he not leave the ninety-nine and go to the mountains to seek the one that is straying?”Matthew 18:12

Since our launch in February, we've seen God move powerfully through GateCity Buckhead. Our growing community of around 100 members has begun giving generously, many for the very first time as new believers. We're entering our second year full of faith, hoping to balance our budget and reach a sustainable financial place by the end of year three.
