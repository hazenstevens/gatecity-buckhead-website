Service Details

What Sunday looks like.

  • AgesAge 3 through 5th grade
  • ToddlersWe’re actively working to create a dedicated space for children ages 1–2. In the meantime, we may be able to accommodate younger toddlers on a case-by-case basis, depending on our current staff-to-child ratios.
  • TimeSundays at 11:00am. Check-in starts at 10:45am.
  • WhereAtlanta International School, 2890 N Fulton Dr NE
  • What to expectWorship, an engaging Bible lesson, and interactive activities that make God's Word come alive in a way kids actually enjoy.

GateCity Youth

Middle and high school.

We’re creating a space where our youth can grow in the Lord, connect, and build meaningful relationships together.

  • Middle schoolersEvery 2nd and 4th Sunday at 11:00am. Middle school students worship together with the church, then head out after worship for their youth class.
  • High schoolersEvery 2nd Sunday at 10:00am.
Safety & Security

Your child's safety is our highest priority.

Every volunteer is background-checked and trained to provide a safe, attentive environment. From secure check-in and pickup procedures to monitored classrooms, we take every step to give you peace of mind while your children are with us.
