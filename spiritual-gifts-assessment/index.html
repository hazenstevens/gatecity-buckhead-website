Free Spiritual Gifts Assessment

What are my spiritual gifts?

Every follower of Jesus has been given gifts by God's grace, to build others up and point them to Him. This free spiritual gifts test helps you discover yours in about 10 minutes. You will see your top two gifts right away, and we will email you your full results.

Start the Assessment →How It Works
How it works

Three parts, straight from Scripture.

Paul lists spiritual gifts in three places, and each list shows a different side of how God works through His people. This assessment walks through all three.

  • Part 1: Romans 12Gifts of grace. How God has wired you to love and serve: prophecy, serving, teaching, encouragement, giving, leadership and mercy (Romans 12:6-8).
  • Part 2: 1 Corinthians 12Gifts of the Spirit. How the Holy Spirit shows up through you to help others: wisdom, knowledge, faith, healing, miracles, prophecy, discernment, tongues and interpretation (1 Corinthians 12:7-11).
  • Part 3: Ephesians 4Gifts that equip. The five gifts Jesus gave to build up His church: apostle, prophet, evangelist, shepherd and teacher (Ephesians 4:11-13).

Peter sums them all up: "As each has received a gift, employ it in serving one another, as good managers of the grace of God" (1 Peter 4:10, WEB). Some gifts are speaking gifts and some are serving gifts, and every one of them is for others.

You will rate 54 short statements from "Not like me" to "Very much like me." Answer from your life right now, not where you hope to be. There are no wrong answers. Think of your results as a starting place for a conversation with God and your community, not a label.
