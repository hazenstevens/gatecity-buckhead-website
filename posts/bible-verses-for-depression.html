If depression and heaviness are closing in around you, you are not alone, and it does not mean your faith has failed. God is the God of all of life: body, mind and spirit. Sometimes the help He gives is spiritual, sometimes it is physical, and often it is both. Here are the verses that have carried our family through dark seasons, and a few honest words about walking through them.

If you are in crisis or thinking about ending your life, please reach out now. In the U.S. you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline any time, day or night. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Bible Verses for Depression and Heaviness

When you feel weighed down

to provide for those who mourn in Zion, to give to them a garland for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness; that they may be called trees of righteousness, the planting of Yahweh, that he may be glorified.

Isaiah 61:3, World English Bible

Isaiah calls it "the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness." God does not shame us for the heaviness. He offers to exchange it, beauty for ashes and joy for mourning, as we turn toward Him.

When you are worn out

“Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.”

Matthew 11:28-30, World English Bible

When your soul needs restoring

Yahweh is my shepherd: I shall lack nothing. He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside still waters. He restores my soul. He guides me in the paths of righteousness for his name’s sake.

Psalms 23:1-3, World English Bible

Notice who does the work in this psalm. The Shepherd makes us lie down. He leads. He restores. In seasons of heaviness, sometimes the most faithful thing we can do is let Him.

When your heart is broken

Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.

Psalms 34:18, World English Bible

When you need to talk back to your own soul

Why are you in despair, my soul? Why are you disturbed within me? Hope in God! For I shall still praise him, the saving help of my countenance, and my God.

Psalms 42:11, World English Bible

A prayer for hope

Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that you may abound in hope, in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Romans 15:13, World English Bible

Romans 15:13 is a prayer you can pray over yourself every morning: God of hope, fill me with joy and peace as I trust You, so that I overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.

When Grief Became Heaviness

One of the most depressed seasons of my life came after my father's sudden and unexpected death. Grief and sorrow filled me to the point that I lost my appetite and lost my desire for things I normally enjoyed. I learned that you cannot simply pray away that kind of grief.

Prayer was part of how God carried me, and often the foundation of it. But His wisdom included a whole-life approach. A fellowship of men who understood grief walked with me through it. And my wife Hannah's kindness and love toward me in my weakness was a gift from God I will never forget.

God Under the Bed

Hannah has walked through her own season of depression. She tells the story in her book The Surrendered Mind:

It began like every other day for weeks. I was depressed, beyond hope, and hiding, miserably curled up under the bed. I hadn't thought about God in over six weeks. But in that moment, tucked beneath the frame and lost in the dark, a thought of Him flickered across my mind.

Guilt and shame surged like a flood. I hadn't prayed. I hadn't read Scripture. I hadn't done any of the "checklist" things that were supposed to keep my faith intact. And then, from the depths of that shame, a pathetic but honest prayer rose from within me: "God, I'm sorry I forgot you. I'm sorry I haven't done any of the things. I just can't right now."

In an instant, He was there. The Presence of God surrounded me, under the bed, in the darkness, in my despair. And to my shock, I didn't feel His disappointment. I felt His delight. Then came His voice. Not loud. Not dramatic. Just a gentle whisper you can't help but believe: "Hannah, I don't care about any of those things. I just want you."

Hannah Stevens, The Surrendered Mind

If you can't pray right now, that honest sentence is enough. God is not waiting for you to finish a checklist. He is already with you, even in the darkest hiding place, and He wants you.

God Is the God of All of Life

When we fight depression or heaviness, it helps to know there can be physical dimensions and there can be dimensions of the soul. God cares about both.

Look at how He cared for Elijah. After a great victory, Elijah ran into the wilderness so discouraged that he asked to die. God did not lecture him. An angel woke him with food and water and let him sleep again.

He lay down and slept under a juniper tree; and behold, an angel touched him, and said to him, “Arise and eat!” He looked, and behold, there was at his head a cake baked on the coals, and a jar of water. He ate and drank, and lay down again. Yahweh’s angel came again the second time, and touched him, and said, “Arise and eat, because the journey is too great for you.”

1 Kings 19:5-7, World English Bible

Rest, food, people who love you, and wise help from doctors and counselors are not signs of weak faith. They can be the very way God restores your soul. If heaviness has stayed with you for weeks, please talk with a doctor or a licensed counselor, and let a pastor or trusted friend walk with you too.

Does Depression Mean My Faith Is Weak?

No. Paul wrote about a "thorn in the flesh" that afflicted him. We don't know what it was, but we know he asked God three times to take it away. God's answer was not a rebuke for weak faith:

He has said to me, “My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.” Most gladly therefore I will rather glory in my weaknesses, that the power of Christ may rest on me.

2 Corinthians 12:9, World English Bible

James adds that the testing of our faith is not a sign that faith is missing. It is how faith grows:

Count it all joy, my brothers, when you fall into various temptations, knowing that the testing of your faith produces endurance. Let endurance have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing.

James 1:2-4, World English Bible

So if you are in a dark season, don't let anyone condemn you for weak faith, including the voice in your own head. God is growing your faith step by step as you walk with Him, and leaning on Him in your weakness is exactly where His strength shows up.

You Were Not Meant to Carry This Alone

Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.

Galatians 6:2, World English Bible

Tell someone. Let a friend, a small group, a pastor or a counselor into what you are carrying. Keep praying, even if your prayers are only one word. Keep reading the Psalms, which are full of people who felt exactly what you feel and still turned toward God. And hold onto this: the God of hope is near to the brokenhearted, and He has not let go of you.
