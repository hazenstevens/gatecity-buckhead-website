The best way to pray for your daughter is to pray God's own words over her life, specifically and consistently. You do not need perfect words. You need a few clear prayers rooted in Scripture that you can return to every day, through every season of her life.

Our daughters are growing up surrounded by voices telling them who to be and what they are worth. Prayer is how we speak a truer word over them, first to God and then to them.

Then our sons will be like well-nurtured plants, our daughters like pillars carved to adorn a palace.

Psalm 144:12, World English Bible

10 Prayers for Your Daughter

1. That she would know Jesus for herself

Pray that her faith would become her own, not just something she grew up around.

All your children will be taught by Yahweh; and your children's peace will be great.

Isaiah 54:13

Pray: "Lord, teach my daughter Yourself. Let her know You as her own Father and Friend, and fill her with Your peace."

2. That she would know she is wonderfully made

Few prayers matter more in this generation. Pray that your daughter's sense of worth would be anchored in God's design, not in comparison.

I will give thanks to you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Your works are wonderful. My soul knows that very well.

Psalm 139:14

Pray: "God, let my daughter's soul know very well that she is fearfully and wonderfully made. Silence every lie about her worth."

3. For a beauty that starts in the heart

...the hidden person of the heart, in the incorruptible adornment of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is in the sight of God very precious.

1 Peter 3:4

Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain; but a woman who fears Yahweh, she shall be praised.

Proverbs 31:30

Pray: "Lord, grow a beauty in my daughter that does not fade. Let her love You more than she loves being admired."

4. For strength and dignity

God does not want our daughters fragile or fearful. He wants them clothed in strength.

Strength and dignity are her clothing. She laughs at the time to come. She opens her mouth with wisdom. Faithful instruction is on her tongue.

Proverbs 31:25-26

Pray: "Father, clothe my daughter in strength and dignity. Let her face the future with joy instead of fear, and let her words carry wisdom."

5. For a renewed and peaceful mind

Anxiety is one of the heaviest things young women carry today. Pray for her thought life.

Don't be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.

Romans 12:2

Pray: "Holy Spirit, guard my daughter's mind. Replace anxious thoughts with Your truth and Your peace."

6. For good friends

Pray that she would find friends who build her up and point her to Jesus, and that she would be that kind of friend.

Pray: "God, give my daughter faithful friends who love You. Protect her from relationships that would pull her away from You."

7. For a pure and guarded heart

Keep your heart with all diligence, for out of it is the wellspring of life.

Proverbs 4:23

Pray: "Lord, guard my daughter's heart. Help her give it only to those who will honor it, and keep her close to You."

8. That she would believe what God says to her

Mary was called blessed not because life was easy, but because she believed God.

Blessed is she who believed, for there will be a fulfillment of the things which have been spoken to her from the Lord!

Luke 1:45

Pray: "Lord, speak to my daughter, and give her faith to believe what You say. Let her see Your promises fulfilled in her life."

9. For her calling and her voice

Let no man despise your youth; but be an example to those who believe, in word, in your way of life, in love, in spirit, in faith, and in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

Pray: "God, show my daughter the work You made her for. Do not let anyone despise her youth. Let her be an example now."

10. For protection and her future

For he will put his angels in charge of you, to guard you in all your ways.

Psalm 91:11

Pray: "Father, protect my daughter wherever she goes. If You lead her to marriage, prepare a godly husband who will love her as Christ loves the church."

How to Pray When Your Daughter Is Struggling

When your daughter is hurting, anxious or far from God, your prayers may feel like they are not reaching her. They are reaching God. Lamentations gives parents permission to pour it all out:

Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord. Lift up your hands toward him for the life of your young children.

Lamentations 2:19

Pray honestly. Ask God for the right words when you talk with her, and for patience when there are no words. Pray that He would send people into her life who can reach her when you cannot. And keep believing that the God who started a good work in her is still working.

A Short Prayer for My Daughter

Father, thank You for my daughter. She is Your gift, and I give her back to You. Let her know You for herself. Let her soul know that she is fearfully and wonderfully made. Clothe her in strength and dignity, and let her laugh at the days to come. Guard her heart and her mind. Give her faithful friends. Let her believe every word You speak to her, and let her see it fulfilled. Protect her wherever she goes. In Jesus' name, amen.

Make It a Family Rhythm

Pray these out loud over your daughter, not just about her. Bedtime, the drive to school and the dinner table are some of the most powerful places to do it. Scripture pictures faith being passed on in the everyday moments of a home (Deuteronomy 6:6-7). A simple way to start is the priestly blessing from Numbers 6, which I walk through in The Blessing Prayer.
