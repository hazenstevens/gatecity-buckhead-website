If you have put your faith in Jesus, yes, the Holy Spirit lives in you. He comes to dwell in every believer at new birth (John 3:5-8), and Paul says plainly that your body is "a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you" (1 Corinthians 6:19). He does not merely visit you or hover near you. He moves in.

That is the most staggering claim Christianity makes about an ordinary human being. The Spirit who hovered over the waters at creation, who rested on Jesus at the Jordan, the very Person of God, takes up residence inside ordinary people. From that moment your own body becomes a place where the presence of God dwells on the earth.

That truth is too large to take in all at once, so let me build it the way Scripture does: what begins to flow out of you when He comes, what it means that you have become His temple, and what He does once He is there.

Living Water From Within

Jesus described what would happen to anyone who came to Him in thirst.

"If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink! He who believes in me, as the Scripture has said, from within him will flow rivers of living water." But he said this about the Spirit, which those believing in him were to receive.

John 7:37-39, World English Bible

Notice the size of the promise. He does not say trickles will flow from your heart. He does not say little streams, or that you will have puddles collecting somewhere inside you. He says rivers, rivers of living water flowing from your innermost being.

And those rivers are not meant to terminate on you. The river flows through me and into my family. It flows over my wife when I pray for her healing. It reaches my children in our moments of intercession together. The Spirit was never meant to be a private reservoir. He was meant to be a current that runs through your life and out into everyone around you.

The receiving begins at new birth, and it is received by faith in Jesus.

Most certainly I tell you, unless one is born of water and spirit, he can't enter into God's Kingdom. That which is born of the flesh is flesh. That which is born of the Spirit is spirit.

John 3:5-6, World English Bible

But here is what many believers miss. New birth is the doorway, not the destination. The Holy Spirit does not come in once and then go dormant. We are welcomed into a life of ongoing encounter with the Person of the Holy Spirit.

You Are the Temple

To feel the weight of what it means that He lives in you, go back to the day Solomon's temple was dedicated. As the singers and trumpeters lifted one sound of praise, the glory arrived like weather.

The house was filled with a cloud, even Yahweh's house, so that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud; for Yahweh's glory filled God's house.

2 Chronicles 5:13-14, World English Bible

The priests could not even stay on their feet to do their work. The presence was too heavy. That is what it looked like when God filled His house.

Now hold that whole picture up against what Paul says you have become.

Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God.

1 Corinthians 6:19, World English Bible

The word he uses is naos. Not the sprawling temple complex with its outer courts, but the inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place itself, the single room where the glory rested. Paul is telling you that the dwelling place of God has relocated. You do not travel to a building to stand near the presence anymore. You are the room the fire fell in.

Peter gathers up the same picture: "You also, as living stones, are built up as a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood" (1 Peter 2:5). Living stones in a living temple, with a holy priesthood serving in it. That is who you are.

The Helper Who Demonstrates

Jesus called the Spirit the Paraclete: the Helper, the Counselor, the Advocate. Every word of that title is good news. He teaches us and leads us into truth. He comforts us in our pain and helps us comfort others. He conforms us to the image of Christ. He helps us pray when we have no words.

But the Helper does not only teach and comfort. He also demonstrates.

I will never forget a season when I was ministering weekly in a jail. There was one man being discipled among us who was sincere but unconvinced, genuinely unsure whether God was real. One day, as the guards were already moving to escort me out, I noticed him limping. The Holy Spirit pressed it on my heart: pray for him. I knelt down, laid my hand on his knee, and prayed all of three seconds: "Lord, heal his knee."

He stopped me. While intoxicated he had been in a hit-and-run and badly hurt someone else, and the guilt was crushing him. "My injury is my penalty for what I did wrong," he said. "Don't pray for me."

I looked him in the eye and said, "God didn't give you this injury to punish you. He is merciful." Then I was gone. Neither of us felt anything in that moment.

The next week he told me what had happened. He had laid down in his bunk that night and felt heat pulsing through his leg all night long. He woke up the next morning and the leg was healed. It had been broken and never properly treated, and he had been told it would never heal. Now it was whole, with no pain when he walked.

But here is what he said to me, and it is the part I carry in my soul: "More precious than my leg being healed is that I now know, without a doubt, that God is real. That is the greater gift."

Start Talking to Him

He has come to live in you. He is not a doctrine to be admired or a memory to be revisited, but a Person in residence: the breath that made you new, the river in your innermost being, the presence in the temple He has made of you.

So if you take nothing else from this, take this: start talking to Him. Holy Spirit, what should I do? Holy Spirit, grow love in my heart. Holy Spirit, give me wisdom. Holy Spirit, guide my steps. If you want God to speak to you more, perhaps the key is to begin speaking more to Him. A relationship grows in conversation.

You might even open your hands as you pray, the posture of receiving a gift, and simply say: Holy Spirit, come. Make Yourself known to me as a Person. Fill me with Your love, Your power, and Your truth.
