You are not the only one

You believe. You just don’t know how to stay consistent.

Most of us never meant to drift. Life got busy, a season got hard, church got skipped, and now God feels further away than we want Him to be. You want to walk closely with Him today, and stay on track with Him over the long term.

  • Faith feels flatYou still believe, but you wonder if there is more to following Jesus than getting through another week.
  • Faith feels lonelyA move, a hard season, a packed calendar. Somewhere along the way you lost the people who walked with you.
  • You keep restartingA new plan, a fresh Monday, a few good weeks. Then it fades. You want something that actually lasts.
The life Jesus offers

Not a better week. A life that flourishes in every season.

Staying on track with God is not about trying harder. It is about staying close to the Shepherd and putting down roots that hold when life shakes.

  • Psalm 1:3“He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water, that brings forth its fruit in its season, whose leaf also does not wither.”
  • John 10:27, 10:10“My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me... I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly.”

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Who is this for?
Anyone who believes in God and wants to walk more closely with Jesus today, and stay on track with Him over the long term. Maybe you are new to faith, coming back after a season away, or you simply want more than getting through another week. You are welcome here.
What is the weekly commitment?
About four hours a week, all online: Sunday worship at 11am, Digital Connect and Sunday Forum from 8 to 9pm on Sundays, a Digital Formation Group on Wednesdays from 8 to 9:15pm, and one prayer meeting of your choice, plus a short daily devotional from the Word connected to the cohort’s theme. The next cohort runs January 10 through late February 2027.
Who else is participating?
Your cohort is a small group of other committed people walking through the same season together, where people know you by name. You will also be joining the wider GateCity Buckhead church community, who take part in the same online prayer meetings, Formation Nights, and Bible studies. The difference is that you will do it alongside a cohort of committed people, while others join simply as members of the community. We completed 3 cohorts in 2026, with more than 15 people finishing the journey, and so far they have come from 7 states, all by word of mouth.
Do I need to be from Atlanta to be a part?
No. Everything happens online, so you can join from anywhere in the world. So far, participants have joined from Florida, Georgia, Minnesota, Missouri, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Texas.
What if I attend GateCity in person? Can I join the digital cohort too?
Yes. Many people who worship with us in Buckhead on Sundays join the cohort for the community, the formation group, and the rhythms of prayer during the week.
What does it cost, and why is it free?
It is free. We believe everyone should have the chance to walk closely with Jesus, and cost should never be the thing that holds someone back. The cohort is made possible by the generosity of the GateCity Buckhead community and our partners.
Who are the leaders?

Hazen Stevens, Lead Pastor. Hazen thrives on seeing lives transformed by Jesus. He and Hannah lead GateCity Buckhead, a church in Buckhead, Atlanta, with a heart for prayer, presence, and helping people walk with Jesus for the whole of life. He loves creating meaningful experiences that connect people with God and each other.

Hannah Stevens, Co-Pastor, Spiritual Formation and Small Groups. Hannah loves creating spaces where people can slow down and deeply connect with God and others. She leads spiritual formation and small groups at GateCity, and brings warmth, persistence, and a deep love for Jesus to every gathering.

Sarah Pearl Shivers, Associate Pastor of Prayer. Sarah Pearl is passionate about helping people connect deeply with the presence of God through prayer and worship. She started at GateCity Lawrenceville in 2018, served on Prayer Room staff, and joined the Buckhead team in 2025. She loves the sense of family and the growing prayer culture at GateCity.

Karissa Corpeny, Director of Systems and Processes. Karissa helps the cohort run smoothly behind the scenes, so you can focus on growing with Jesus and your group. She loves being part of a presence-centered church where faith comes alive.
