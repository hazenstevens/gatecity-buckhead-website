Digital Discipleship Cohort

Walk with Jesus, and stay on track.

You believe. You just feel a little off track. The cohort is a shared journey of relationships, prayer, and simple rhythms that help you follow Jesus in everyday life, all online, wherever you live.

You are not the only one

Most of us never meant to drift.

Life got busy, a season got hard, church got skipped, and now God feels further away than we want Him to be.

  • Faith feels flatYou still believe, but you wonder if there is more to following Jesus than getting through another week.
  • Faith feels lonelyA move, a hard season, a packed calendar. Somewhere along the way you lost the people who walked with you.
  • You keep restartingA new plan, a fresh Monday, a few good weeks. Then it fades. You want something that actually lasts.
The life Jesus offers

Not a better week. A life that flourishes in every season.

Staying on track with God is not about trying harder. It is about staying close to the Shepherd and putting down roots that hold when life shakes.

  • Psalm 1:3“He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water, that brings forth its fruit in its season, whose leaf also does not wither.”
  • John 10:27, 10:10“My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me... I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly.”
Four weekly rhythms

About four hours a week. All online.

You belong and grow at GateCity Buckhead, even if you cannot be with us in person.

Sundays · 11:00am

Worship (Online)

Watch the service and engage with the teaching.

Sundays · 8:00–9:00pm

Digital Connect + Sunday Forum

Catch up in the digital lobby, then a Discovery Bible Study together.

Wednesdays · 8:00–9:15pm

Digital Formation Group

A small group for guided discussion, Scripture, and spiritual practices.

One per week

Selected Prayer Meeting

Join one recurring virtual prayer gathering for shared prayer and worship.

What holds it together

Known by name. Growing on purpose.

  • CommunityA small group where you are known by name.
  • FormationRhythms of prayer and Scripture, with clear, simple commitments.
  • Check-inA weekly “State of Your Soul” check-in to stay honest and growing.
  • PracticeServing, reaching, and generosity, lived out where you are, plus optional gatherings and challenges.
