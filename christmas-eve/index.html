Christmas Eve · Thursday, December 24

Christmas Eve Candlelight Service.

A family-friendly candlelight service at 5:00pm, where kids and parents worship together to classic Christmas songs and modern favorites. Then stay for Christmas cookies. Everyone is welcome, and you don’t need to know anyone to come.

Save Your Spot → Directions & Parking
What to Expect

An hour to slow down and behold Jesus.

  • WhenThursday, December 24, at 5:00pm.
  • How longAbout one hour, followed by Christmas cookies.
  • WorshipClassic Christmas carols and some modern favorites, sung together by candlelight.
  • MessageA brief homily, a short reflective message on the joy and meaning of Christmas.
  • WhereAtlanta International School, secondary school building, 2890 N Fulton Dr NE, Atlanta, GA 30305.
  • ParkingFree, right in front of the building.
  • What to wearBusiness casual, but if you want to dress up for Christmas, that is welcome!
  • FamiliesKids stay with their parents and worship together, so the whole family experiences Christmas Eve side by side. Grandparents welcome too.
  • AfterStay for Christmas cookies and meet the GateCity Buckhead family.
